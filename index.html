Boulder, Colorado Flood: How the City's Resilience Strategy Saved It

The 2013 intense week-long rainstorm could have been devastating for Boulder, Colorado, but the city fared relatively well. Thanks to city planning and floodplain management, the city was damaged but not destroyed. For more information about this video, visit Still Life Projects .

Closed captions are available in English, Español, and Français for this video.  Please use the settings button from within the video to enable captions.
